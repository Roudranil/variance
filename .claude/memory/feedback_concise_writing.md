Be miserly with words in all technical documentation. Write only what is required.

**Why:** The founder found the SDS sections (written 2026-04-20) too verbose — too many characters, over-explained rationale, redundant prose.

**How to apply:**
- Decision only. Reason optional, 3–5 words max. "UUID. Sync-friendly." enough.
- Tables and bullets. No prose. No summaries.
- No restatements. No intros. No closings.
- When in doubt, cut it.
- Applies to all agents writing docs.
